## About Me

`  Ever since I was a kid, I was fascinated by how computers worked and how to write code. That curiosity led me to pursue a degree in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago, where I quickly channeled my passion into hands-on experience. Outside of the classroom, I spent my free time building personal projects like a cryptocurrency tracker and immersing myself in clubs centered around web development. Last summer, that drive took me further as I had the opportunity to work alongside my professor on Curio, a collaborative visual analytics framework designed for urban designers. There, I built testing infrastructure, developed a Jupyter Notebook import system for cross-framework compatibility, and integrated LLM API calls to streamline the notebook migration process.

    Currently, I am leading the Web Development Special Interest Group at UIC's ACM chapter, where I teach beginners the fundamentals of React, Flask, and Postgresql. Beyond teaching, our team is building a platform that catalogs every club UIC has to offer, allowing students to discover organizations, track their memberships, and manage their schedules all in one place. On the side, I am also developing a browser-based Tilt-A-Maze game that incorporates user authentication and database management. These projects reflect my commitment to building software that is not only technically sound but also genuinely useful to the people around me.

    Looking ahead, I am actively seeking a Summer 2027 internship where I can apply my full-stack skills in a professional setting and continue growing as a developer. I am open to a variety of roles and industries, as what matters most to me is being in an environment where I can learn, contribute meaningfully, and be challenged. My long-term goal is to secure an entry-level software engineering position after graduation and steadily work my way toward a senior role. I am also eager to expand my professional network and connect with engineers, developers, and tech professionals who are passionate about building impactful software. If any of that resonates with you, I would love to connect.`
